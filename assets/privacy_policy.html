1 Information We Collect

1.1 Account Information

When you create a SwiftCall account, we collect:

  • Full name — used to identify you to other users.
  • Email address — used for authentication and account recovery.
  • Profile photo (optional) — displayed to contacts you communicate with.
  • Phone number (optional) — if you choose to add it to your profile.
  • Google account data — if you sign in via Google Sign-In, we receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google.

1.2 Messages & Chat Content

When you use SwiftCall's messaging features, we store the following content in Firebase Cloud Firestore:

  • Text messages, replies, and reactions.
  • Images, videos, audio voice messages, and files you send or receive — stored in Firebase Storage.
  • Delivery and read status (read receipts).
  • Typing indicators (ephemeral — not stored permanently).
  • Location data (latitude / longitude) — only if you choose to share your location in a chat.
⚠️  Message Encryption

Messages are transmitted over encrypted HTTPS connections. However, message content is stored in Firebase Firestore and is not end-to-end encrypted by default. Do not share highly sensitive information through the app.

1.3 Call Data

  • Call type (voice or video), date, time, and duration.
  • Caller and receiver identifiers.
  • Call status (answered, missed, rejected, cancelled).

Audio and video streams during calls are processed by LiveKit (see Section 4). We do not record or store the actual audio or video content of your calls.

1.4 Device & Technical Data

  • FCM Token — a device-specific Firebase Cloud Messaging token used to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • VoIP Token (iOS only) — an Apple PushKit token used to deliver incoming call alerts when the app is closed.
  • Online status — whether you are currently active in the app and your last-seen timestamp.
  • App settings — language preference and dark/light mode, stored locally on your device.

1.5 Friend & Contact Relationships

We store your friend list, incoming friend requests, and outgoing friend requests in Firestore so that contacts can be synchronized across your devices.

We do not sell your personal data to third parties, nor do we use it for targeted advertising beyond what is described in Section 5 (Unity Ads).

4 Calls & LiveKit

Voice and video calls in SwiftCall are powered by LiveKit, an open-source WebRTC infrastructure provider. When you make or receive a call:

  • Your audio and/or video stream is transmitted in real time directly to the other participant through LiveKit's servers using end-to-end encrypted WebRTC technology.
  • SwiftCall does not record, store, or retain the audio or video content of any call.
  • LiveKit uses SRTP (Secure Real-time Transport Protocol) to encrypt media streams in transit.
  • To join a call room, we exchange a short-lived JWT token with our backend server (hosted on Render.com). This token includes your user ID and the room name but does not include your message content.
  • Call metadata (duration, type, participants) is recorded in Firestore for your call history and token charging purposes.
🔒  WebRTC & TURN Servers

To establish calls across different networks, the app may use STUN/TURN relay servers. These relay encrypted media packets and do not have access to the content of your conversations.

For group calls, the same infrastructure applies. All participants are authenticated before joining a group call room.

5 Advertising & Unity Ads

SwiftCall displays advertisements served by Unity Ads (Unity Technologies). These ads help us keep the app free for all users.

Ad Formats Used

  • Banner Ads — displayed at the bottom of the main screen and settings.
  • Interstitial Ads — displayed after a call ends.
  • Rewarded Ads — optional ads you can watch to earn in-app tokens.

Data Collected by Unity Ads

Unity Ads may collect the following data from your device for the purpose of serving and measuring advertisements:

  • Advertising ID (GAID on Android, IDFA on iOS) — subject to your device privacy settings.
  • Device model, operating system version, and language.
  • IP address (used to infer approximate country/region).
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, completion rate).
📲  Opt-Out of Personalized Ads

You can limit ad tracking at any time through your device settings:
• Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads → Opt out of Ads Personalization.
• iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking → disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

For more information about Unity's data practices, please visit: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy

6 Token Wallet & Transactions

SwiftCall includes an in-app token economy. Tokens are a virtual currency with no real-world monetary value. We collect and store:

  • Token balance — your current token count.
  • Transaction history — a log of how tokens were earned (welcome bonus, watching ads, sharing the app) and spent (calls, messages, media uploads).
  • Daily usage limits — counters for daily ad views and share rewards to prevent abuse.

All token data is stored in Firebase Firestore under a private collection accessible only to your authenticated account. Token packages (if applicable) may be available for purchase in the future; payment processing will be handled by Google Play / Apple App Store and subject to their respective privacy policies.

7 Data Storage & Retention

Where Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on Google Firebase servers, primarily in the United States. Firebase applies appropriate security measures, including encryption at rest and in transit, and complies with GDPR, CCPA, and other applicable data protection regulations.

How Long We Keep Your Data

  • Account data — retained for as long as your account is active.
  • Messages — retained until you or the other party deletes them, or until the conversation is deleted.
  • Call history — retained until you clear your call history within the app.
  • Uploaded media (photos, audio, files) — retained in Firebase Storage until the associated message or conversation is deleted.
  • Token transactions — retained for the lifetime of your account for transparency purposes.
  • FCM / VoIP tokens — updated on each app launch and removed upon account deletion.

Upon account deletion (see Your Rights), we will delete or anonymize all personal data within 30 days, except where retention is required by law.

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data by:

  1. Opening SwiftCall → Settings → Delete Account.
  2. Alternatively, emailing us at jaradatabdullah122@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".

Upon account deletion:

  • Your Firebase Authentication account is permanently removed.
  • Your Firestore profile, messages, call history, friend relationships, and token wallet are deleted.
  • Uploaded media files in Firebase Storage are removed.
  • FCM and VoIP tokens are invalidated.

Deletion is completed within 30 days. Some anonymized aggregate data (e.g., statistical usage counts) may be retained longer but will not be linked to your identity.

In-App Controls

  • Delete a message — long-press any message → Delete.
  • Delete a conversation — long-press a chat → Delete Chat.
  • Clear call history — Calls tab → sweep icon → Clear All.
  • Update profile — Settings → edit name, photo, or phone number.

11 Security

We implement the following security measures to protect your data:

  • TLS/HTTPS — all data transmitted between the app and Firebase services is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.2+.
  • SRTP — all call audio and video is encrypted using Secure Real-time Transport Protocol via LiveKit/WebRTC.
  • Firebase Security Rules — Firestore and Storage access is governed by security rules that ensure each user can only access their own data or data shared with them.
  • Firebase Authentication — all API access requires a valid authenticated session token.
  • Encryption at Rest — Firebase encrypts all stored data at rest by default.
⚠️  No System Is Perfect

While we strive to protect your data, no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure. We cannot guarantee absolute security. If you believe your account has been compromised, please contact us immediately.
